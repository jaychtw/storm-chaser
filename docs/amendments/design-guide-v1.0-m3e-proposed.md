# Proposed Design Guide amendment v1.0.2 — experience-layer innovation

2026-10-29 Asia/Taipei. **Proposed specification text; permanent feature adoption pending.** Basis: [M3-E owner assignment](../briefs/m3-e-authorization.md). The experience priority and bounded POC authorization below are already approved; the owner has not yet accepted the resulting interaction for production integration.

## Already approved direction

Originality of presentation and interaction is more important than novelty of underlying information. Storm Chaser should create preference through a distinctive, intuitive experience of existing lawfully reusable public meteorological evidence. Firsthand observation, proprietary weather procurement and independent forecasting are not product objectives. This refines priority; it does not cancel original functionality or source obligations.

M3-P's visual design at `12dc2fc23ded833e8ba539fddd67caddde2af477` is owner-accepted. That acceptance is separate from reported technical results, real operational evidence, public readiness and visual acceptance of M3-E.

## Proposed additions to Guide §§1, 3 and 9

- Experience promise: **same evidence, new perspective, better experience**. Evaluate originality through exploration, comprehension and evidence access, not animation or feature count alone.
- Candidate signature interaction: **Forecast-Time Explorer**, navigating the actual discrete analyzed/forecast points within a single currently selected advisory. A chosen forecast time synchronizes map emphasis and native source context; analysis and prediction remain distinct.
- Use discreet stepping with real valid times, lead hours and publication gaps. No interpolated official positions, moving storm simulation, confidence/impact calculations or intensity conversions. Keep camera interaction under user control.
- Provide keyboard, mouse and touch navigation, both locales, no dependence on animation, and honest missing/stale/expired behavior. Preserve map-first Night Operations composition and a route to full official evidence.
- This interaction is not historical issuance-cycle browsing. Original cycle-browser deferral, neutral uncertain Auto, manual selection and eventual Taiwan relevance objective remain in force.

## Unchanged authority and boundaries

Original [Guide v1.0](../Storm_Chaser_Design_Guide_v1.0.md), [M3-P amendment](design-guide-v1.0-m3p.md), datum/reuse/provenance constraints and conditional multi-agency objective are retained. No new provider, dependency, asset pipeline, backend, infrastructure activation, expenditure, main merge or deployment. Secondary experiential opportunities remain proposals; the owner decides whether to accept, revise or reject this POC before further integration.

No text in the original guide, historical evidence or governing standards is silently replaced. If adopted, record accepted paragraphs/feature status and a superseding decision; do not mark this entire draft approved merely because the experience priority was approved.
