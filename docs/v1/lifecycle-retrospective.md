# Lifecycle retrospective v1.0 — evidence and destinations

2026-10-29, Asia/Taipei. Current lifecycle **closed and owner-accepted** at [`475dd767d6c18544996b4b9f0204b39dc8c01e3c`](https://github.com/jaychtw/storm-chaser/commit/475dd767d6c18544996b4b9f0204b39dc8c01e3c); [acceptance record](closure-v1.0.md). Documentation retrospective only, governed by the project's pinned AICP 1.1.0 §§5–9. Evidence below is inspected project history and previously reported execution, not newly repeated tests or an independent user study.

## Consequential lessons

| Lesson | Observed evidence and consequence | Classification and bounded destination |
|---|---|---|
| Track milestone objectives separately from the work a later brief authorizes. | Original [Guide §12](../Storm_Chaser_Design_Guide_v1.0.md#12-implementation-milestones-for-codex) called M3 comparison/relevance; delivered [M3-O](../m3-verification.md) hardened JMA operations. [Reconciliation](../reviews/m3-reconciliation.md) exposed the mismatch rather than deleting either record. [ADR 0002](../adr/0002-product-reconciliation-proposed.md) preserved lineage and authorized M3-P repairs. | **Reusable engineering practice.** Propose one conditional brief field for scope differences (handoff S-02). AICP §§3/5/6 already require explicit conflicts/decisions; no mandatory new reconciliation document. |
| A bounded freeze can define a complete product without pretending blocked goals were delivered. | [ADR 0004](../adr/0004-v1-scope-freeze.md) and [GDD v1.0.3](../amendments/design-guide-v1.0.3-scope-freeze.md) accepted the time explorer and deliberately deferred ranking, extra feeds and broader experiences with [reopening criteria](deferred-capabilities.md). V1-F then assessed a finite scope. This history supports the outcome here, not a measured causal productivity claim. | **Project-specific.** Keep frozen scope/register authoritative; do not make early feature freezing a universal creativity rule. |
| Tests, feature acceptance, software acceptance and deployment are different decisions. | [M3-E report](../m3e-review.md) delivered 110 reported tests while feature adoption still awaited review. V1-F authorized adoption, then [131 tests/53 scans](verification.md) supported review. The later explicit [owner closure assignment, recorded here](closure-v1.0.md) accepted the software. No stage authorized public operation. | **Project-specific application of existing AICP.** Preserve separate states and acceptance provenance; no new protocol gate needed. A small state-template example can represent closed local software (S-04). |
| Retained client data needs a validity clock independent of successful refresh. | The historical [+25h probe](../reviews/m3-expiry-probe.json) retained one system/four forecasts on the client while the server excluded the system. [M3-P](../m3p-verification.md) shared eligibility and tested failed refresh plus resume; [V1-F](verification.md) rejected malformed replies without renewing last-good data. Future-valid points cannot override an expired parent bulletin. | **Reusable engineering practice**, when displaying expiring external data. Retain the outage/clock-jump/resume technique in technical examples, not a universal 8h/24h threshold or new AICP rule. Stale, expired and archived have different meanings. |
| Startup side effects must be explicit, especially during safe local review. | At [M3-E source](https://github.com/jaychtw/storm-chaser/blob/129a3af3a13e882d8d92bcfd0360fdfa83b01cf7/server/index.ts), API startup could invoke a collector/timer unless externally scheduled. [V1-F](verification.md) removed the embedded collector and [package checks](package-verification.json) used a sentinel to verify the reader did not invoke collection. Read-only describes reader/collection behavior; local archive initialization is still documented, not a claim of zero filesystem writes. | **Reusable engineering practice** for networked/background applications. Propose a conditional startup-side-effects field in an existing technical brief (S-03), not a rule that every game needs a scheduler/service architecture. |
| Preserve accepted design across handoffs by linking authority, not reconstructing it from memory. | [ADR 0003](../adr/0003-experience-layer-poc.md) retained owner-accepted Night Operations design and an experience-first direction. The proposed M3-E wording was explicitly adopted by [v1.0.3](../amendments/design-guide-v1.0.3-scope-freeze.md). V1-F used the state index and did not restart design exploration. | **Reusable engineering practice**, already supported by AICP §§4/5/7 and the state template. Keep references compact. No new mandatory transcript export or persistent AI memory claim. Separate ChatGPT Project history was not retrieved. |
| Experiential originality can reorganize trusted information without inventing source data. | [M3-E rationale and matched renders](../m3e-review.md) show chronological stepping through the same JMA points; explicit irregular gaps, analysis/prediction labels and stable camera avoid simulated motion. V1-F integrated that interaction without a new provider. | **Project-specific.** Preserve the accepted experience philosophy. No evidence that every product needs a timeline, or that human comprehension/engagement was quantitatively measured. |
| A $0 constraint changes the delivery claim as well as infrastructure choices. | [ADR 0002 D-005](../adr/0002-product-reconciliation-proposed.md), V1-F's local-first freeze, [cached clean install](clean-install.json) and [separate service checklist](public-service-readiness.md) use existing resources. Historical host estimates never became procurement authority. Local replay avoided source traffic; skipped remote CI was not claimed passed. | **Project-specific constraint.** Continue $0 and no enrollments/activation. Existing resources and free tiers are not proof of continuous free hosting; public operation remains separately authorized/evidenced. |
| Templates must express their intended project type. | AICP §1 covers games/apps, but the inspected `CODEX-BRIEF` and `GDD-TEMPLATE` still default to game-wide UGDS obligations. Storm Chaser did not adopt them mechanically; the [earlier proposal](../proposals/standards-applicability-and-milestone-lineage.md) identifies the wording conflict. | **Reusable engineering practice.** Small conditional template wording (S-01), preserving every adopting game's UGDS requirements. No broad UGDS waiver or retroactive app adoption. |
| Report what a measurement actually establishes. | [Offline audit](security.json) did not establish current advisories. [Clean-install/build results](clean-install.json) were same-host reproducibility. [Performance](performance.json) measured 1.18–2.23s local versus 7.48–8.27s constrained map readiness with only two samples/profile/software WebGL. | **Reusable engineering practice.** Keep environment/freshness/sample limits beside results; optimize against an approved delivery target. Existing AICP §6 evidence labels suffice; no universal bundle limit, new framework or external audit service proposed. |

## Consolidation, not automatic promotion

No observed lesson requires a new **AICP collaboration improvement** to the core protocol: §§3–9 already cover scope conflicts, owner decisions, handoff, evidence, separate gates and proportionate retrospectives. The strongest gap is applicability wording in templates; a closed-local phase example and two conditional brief fields are smaller than expanding AICP. The [consolidated handoff](../proposals/closure-standards-handoff.md) provides exact candidate wording, evidence, tradeoffs, approval and versioning boundaries.

The inspected newer standards revision adds portfolio-publication guidance, but Storm Chaser has no public release and its pinned version is unchanged. No portal entry or shared-standard implementation follows. No game-quality amendment is supported by this project alone. Future evidence may justify a technical example for expiring data; it does not yet justify a compulsory cross-project process.

The current cycle has no outstanding acceptance condition. Future owners may choose an enhancement or public operating model with explicit scope, evidence and authorization; closure does not execute that choice. Preserve the product, its limitations and the useful lessons without restarting development.
